Type into other Windows apps

Download the helper and run it once. After that you do not need this page: click into any app and press Ctrl Alt D. A small mic window appears in the corner and types what you say; press it again to stop. The switch above is only for dictating from this page instead.

Type into other Android apps

Download the Amirus Dictator keyboard (29 KB). Install it, follow its three set-up steps, then pick it as your keyboard in any app and tap its mic.
